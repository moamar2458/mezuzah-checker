Train the letter AI

The AI learns the 27 shapes of ktav ashuri (22 letters + ך ם ן ף ץ). It learns from:

  1. Your library — letters from real mezuzot (exported from the phone with “Export library”).
  2. Font letters — letters drawn from two free STaM fonts, so the AI has a start even before you have many mezuzot.

Everything runs inside this browser. Nothing is uploaded. On a Windows PC: open this page in Chrome or Edge.
